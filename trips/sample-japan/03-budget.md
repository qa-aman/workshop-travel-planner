## If over budget, cut here (in order)
1. Move Tokyo stay from Ginza or Shibuya to Asakusa, saves about $40 per night.
2. Drop one sit-down dinner per day for street food or convenience store meals, saves about $15 to $20 per day.
3. Book non-reserved Shinkansen cars or travel off-peak, saves about $10 to $15 per journey.
4. Skip optional paid temple or shrine add-ons such as special exhibitions, saves about $5 to $10 each.

## If under budget by more than 25%, spend here (in order)
1. Upgrade to reserved Shinkansen seats and add a private airport or station transfer.
2. Add a guided day trip to Nara or Arashiyama with temple access, avoids crowds and adds depth.
3. Upgrade the hotel category or add a kaiseki dinner experience in Kyoto.
