## If over budget, cut here (in order)
1. Move the Tokyo stay from a central area like Ginza or Shibuya to a quieter neighbourhood like Asakusa or Nippori, saves about $30 to $40 per night.
2. Swap one or two sit-down dinners for izakaya or street food (matches the food preference and skips queues), saves about $15 to $20 per day.
3. Book unreserved Shinkansen seats or a local/rapid train for shorter hops instead of reserved seats, saves about $10 to $15 per one-way trip.

## If under budget by more than 25%, spend here (in order)
1. Add a half-day trip to a quieter temple town near Kyoto (Ohara or Arashiyama early morning to dodge crowds), costs about $40 to $70.
2. Upgrade one or two dinners to a kaiseki or omakase experience, matches the food preference, costs about $60 to $100.
3. Book a small-group or private early-access temple visit to avoid crowds, costs about $40 to $70.
