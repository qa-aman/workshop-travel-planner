01 What happened, in order

Six steps, one real incident in the middle

Step 1
Brief written
The orchestrator parsed the request into 00-brief.json: France, Paris + Lyon, 5 days, 1 traveller, $3,000, likes food, avoids crowds, origin Ahmedabad.
Step 2, attempt 1
All three workers came back blocked
destination-research, logistics and budget were launched in parallel and every one refused to invent data, correctly. Their tool list was empty: no search_places, no get_walking_route, no convert_currency. Root cause: the project's travel-tools MCP server was sitting in disabledMcpjsonServers in local settings, and separately had never been added to the trust list Claude Code keeps per project.
Fix
Both trust gates cleared
Cleared the disabled list, then added travel-tools to the project's approved-server list the same way an interactive trust prompt would. claude mcp list flipped from missing entirely, to "pending approval", to ✓ Connected.
Step 2, attempt 2
Workers re-run, all three returned real data
10 candidates per city from destination-research, verified hotel areas and a genuine "no France rail data" finding from logistics, a live FX rate from budget.
Step 3
Draft synthesised
The three worker files were merged into a 5-day draft against the itinerary template, must-do venues placed first, each slot's crowd tactic matched to its time of day.
Step 4
First review: 5 of 6 checks pass
travel_time_realistic failed. 10 of 15 slots had no transit minutes, because synthesis picked venues logistics had never run a walking-route check between.
Step 5
One repair loop, as designed
logistics was re-dispatched to verify seven specific walking legs. It came back with 4 real numbers, and 3 legitimate "could not verify" results, one of them a genuine geocoding bug (see section 4).

Same check failed for an honest reason (no France rail data exists in this build). The skill's rule is one loop only, so the run stopped here and shipped with a Warnings section rather than looping again.
Step 6

03 What got rejected, and why

The system refusing to guess, working as designed

Rejected "Street Food Beyrouth" walking route
get_walking_route resolved the Paris venue name to a point roughly 466 km away, consistent with matching the word "Beyrouth" to Beirut, Lebanon instead of the 75011 Paris address search_places found.
Why: logistics refused to report ~6,390 minutes as a real walking time. The venue stayed on the itinerary (it came from a Paris-biased search), only its two adjacent transit cells were marked could not verify.
Rejected A guessed Paris–Lyon TGV time
get_rail_route's seed file is built from the official JR Central fare table and only covers Japan. No France row exists.
Why: rather than estimate a plausible TGV duration, logistics returned could not verify and told the traveller to confirm directly with SNCF. This is the same design that flagged Tokyo–Kyoto transit for Japan trips, doing exactly its job on a new geography.
Flagged, not blocking travel_time_realistic still fails
With 10 of 15 transit cells honestly unverifiable, the 90-minute check cannot run on 2 of 5 days. The skill allows exactly one repair loop, already used.
Why it still shipped: the three verified days (1, 4, 5) are all comfortably under the limit (51, 65, 39 min), and looping a second time was ruled out by the process, not by the finding.
Found, fixed at source two infrastructure gaps, first non-Japan run
The structural eval's currency check was hardcoded to JPY and would have silently read "FX line: missing" on every future non-Japan trip. A stray semicolon slipped into logistics' own output despite the house rule banning them.
Why it matters: neither had ever been tested against a second country before this run. Both fixed in the same session, logged as D‑029.
